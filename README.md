# Bunique 🧶

### Yarn to Yaari ✨

Hi! Welcome to **Bunique** , my very first startup built with creativity, passion, and lots of yarn. 

Bunique is a handmade crochet brand where every product is carefully crafted with love, warmth, and an aesthetic touch. The idea behind this brand is to turn simple threads into meaningful creations and beautiful connections, from *yarn to yaari*.

##  About Bunique

Starting Bunique is more than just creating a business for me, it’s a dream project and the beginning of my entrepreneurial journey. I wanted to build a brand that feels personal, creative, and comforting while also celebrating handmade art with an Indian touch.

Every crochet piece is handmade with patience, creativity, and attention to detail, making each product unique in its own way.

##  What We Offer

* Handmade crochet products
* Crochet flowers
* Keychains
* Accessories
* Home decor items
